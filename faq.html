General Sourcing Questions

Get quick answers to streamline your procurement process

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

Our MOQ varies by product category:

• Metal Surface Treatment Equipment: Starting from 1 unit for standard models

• Tube Processing Equipment: Typically 1-2 units depending on specifications

• Valve & Pipe Fittings: Bulk orders from 100 pieces recommended for better pricing

• Custom Solutions: Negotiable based on project requirements and specifications

We understand that every business is different. Contact our sales team to discuss MOQ flexibility for your specific needs.

What is the typical Lead Time for delivery?

Production and delivery timelines depend on the product type and order complexity:

• Standard Products (In-stock): 5-10 business days from payment confirmation

• Semi-custom Orders: 15-25 days including production and quality inspection

• Full Custom Solutions: 30-45 days for design review, manufacturing, and testing

• Shipping Time: 7-30 days via Sea freight (depending on destination) or 3-7 days via Air freight

We provide detailed shipping schedules and milestone updates throughout the production cycle. Expedited options available upon request.

What payment methods and terms do you accept?

We offer flexible payment options to accommodate different business requirements:

• 30% Deposit, 70% Before Shipment (Standard for orders above $10,000)

• 50% Deposit, 50% Against B/L Copy (FOB terms)

• 100% T/T Payment (For repeat customers with credit history)

• LC at sight (Letter of Credit accepted for larger orders)

Accepted Payment Methods:

✓ Bank Transfer (T/T) - Most common and fastest
✓ PayPal (for orders under $5,000)
✓ Wire Transfer
✓ Letter of Credit (L/C)

Payment is required to confirmed bank account details. All banking fees are buyer's responsibility unless otherwise negotiated.

What warranty and after-sales support do you provide?

Standard Warranty Coverage:

• 12-Month Warranty: Against manufacturing defects from the shipping date

• 24-Month Warranty: Available for high-value equipment (upgradeable for additional cost)

• Parts Support: Spare parts availability guaranteed for 5 years post-purchase

After-Sales Services:

✓ Free technical consultation and installation guidance
✓ Remote troubleshooting via phone/video call
✓ On-site support available (travel costs negotiable)
✓ Quick response time: 24 hours for urgent issues
✓ Comprehensive operation and maintenance manuals provided

Warranty excludes damage from misuse, improper installation, or normal wear and tear. Extended warranty plans available upon request.

Can you customize products to our specifications?

Yes, we specialize in custom solutions. Our engineering team can work with your specifications:

• Design Modifications: Size, capacity, voltage, material adjustments

• Performance Customization: Speed, pressure, temperature range parameters

• Interface Integration: Custom control systems, PLC integration, automation features

• Branding & Packaging: Custom logos, labels, and packaging options available

Customization Process:

1. Submit detailed specifications and drawings
2. Receive engineering quotation and lead time estimate
3. Design review and approval (up to 2 revision rounds included)
4. Sample production and testing before full order

Minimum order for custom products is typically higher. Please contact us with your requirements for a detailed proposal.

What shipping methods and packaging do you offer?

Shipping Options:

• Sea Freight (FCL/LCL): Most economical, 7-30 days to major ports worldwide

• Air Freight: Faster delivery, 3-7 days, suitable for urgent orders

• Express Courier: Small items only, 1-3 days international delivery

• Rail/Truck: To neighboring Asian countries, cost-effective option

Packaging Standards:

✓ Professional wooden crates for machinery (shock-absorbing padding included)
✓ Weather-resistant packaging for sea transportation
✓ All equipment tested and function verified before packaging
✓ Comprehensive packing list and documentation included
✓ Insurance coverage available (customer's option)

We handle all logistics coordination. Incoterms: FOB Shanghai, CIF, or DDP available. Free shipping insurance for orders over $50,000.

What certifications and quality standards do you have?

International Certifications:

✓ ISO 9001:2015 - Quality Management System
✓ ISO 14001:2015 - Environmental Management
✓ CE Mark - European safety standards compliance
✓ RoHS Compliant - Hazardous substance restrictions
✓ SGS/BV Inspection Report available upon request

Quality Assurance:

• Every equipment undergoes rigorous testing before shipment

• Third-party inspection available (SGS, BV, TUV, etc.)

• Certificates of Origin, Anti-Dumping Documentation available

Our factories are regularly audited and compliant with international trade regulations. We can provide manufacturer certificates for any specific industry requirements.

• Established partnerships with 15+ certified manufacturing facilities

Risk Mitigation:

Company Profile

MachineryHub China is a professional machinery supply chain service provider dedicated to connecting global buyers with China's high-quality manufacturing enterprises. With over 15 years of industry experience, we have provided professional procurement solutions for 500+ medium and large enterprises from Europe, Southeast Asia, and beyond.

We specialize in the machinery supply chain, focusing on the procurement of high-end industrial products such as metal surface treatment equipment, tube processing systems, industrial valves, and fittings. Through long-term strategic partnerships with top domestic manufacturers, we provide our clients with the most competitive products and services.

Our mission is to be your trusted sourcing partner. Through professional supply chain management, rigorous quality control, and efficient project execution, we help you reduce procurement costs, shorten supply cycles, and improve operational efficiency.

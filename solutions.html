🚗 EV Tube Processing Solution

Cutting & Bending Complete Equipment Set for Electric Vehicle Frame Tubes

Solution Overview

Our EV Tube Processing Solution is specifically designed for cutting and bending operations on hollow structural tubes used in electric vehicle frames. This integrated system combines precision cutting, accurate bending, and quality control in a seamless workflow, helping manufacturers achieve consistent tube specifications while reducing waste and production time.

The solution supports various tube materials including steel, aluminum, and stainless steel with tube diameters ranging from 25mm to 120mm, making it versatile for different EV frame designs.

Automatic edge finishing system

✨ Metal Surface Finishing Line

Automatic Polishing & Derusting Line for Round Steel and Bar Stock

Solution Overview

Our Metal Surface Finishing Line delivers fully automated polishing and derusting solutions for round steel, bars, and other linear metal stock. This comprehensive system removes rust, scale, and surface contamination while simultaneously polishing to achieve consistent surface finish quality, significantly improving product appearance and corrosion resistance.

Designed for continuous production with high throughput, the line processes diameter ranges from 8mm to 60mm and can handle various steel grades, stainless steel, and aluminum materials with minimal manual intervention.

Equipment Lineup

🔨 Wire Brush Derusting Module
Multi-spindle brush heads, 100-200 rpm
🪨 Abrasive Polishing Station
Sandpaper and emery cloth options
💧 Washing & Drying System
High-pressure spray and hot air drying
🔄 Continuous Conveyor Track
Variable speed 5-30 m/min, PLC controlled
📐 Automatic Diameter Detection
Laser sensor for adaptive processing
📦 Automatic Stacking System
Bundling and material handling
